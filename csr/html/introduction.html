1. Introduction

Bridging Economy and Ethics in a Globalized World

Background & Motivation

At first glance, economics and ethics are often perceived as two separate segments[cite: 1]. However, business ethics brings these fields closer together[cite: 1]. A central concept shaping this integrated discipline is Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)[cite: 1].

Driven by globalization, our world is changing rapidly[cite: 1]. This transformation introduces escalating challenges for various societal actors, forcing societies to confront major crises such as environmental degradation and demographic shifts[cite: 1]. To overcome these issues, corporations in particular must be willing to take responsibility toward their stakeholders[cite: 1]. Corporate Social Responsibility serves as a guiding framework to support this obligation[cite: 1].

Purpose of this Research

This paper summarizes fundamental aspects of Corporate Social Responsibility[cite: 1]. It is structured to help readers gain an accessible, structured entry point into this extensive topic[cite: 1].
